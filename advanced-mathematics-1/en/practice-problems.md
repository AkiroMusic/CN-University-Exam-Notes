# Advanced Mathematics (Part 1) — Practice Problems

## 1. Limit Computation and Proofs

### (1) Limits of Functions and Sequences

1. $\lim\limits_{n\to\infty}\left(\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{3}{2^2}+\cdots+\dfrac{2n-1}{2^n}\right)$

2. $\lim\limits_{n\to\infty}\left(\sqrt{n+2}-2\sqrt{n+1}+\sqrt{n}\right)$

3. $\lim\limits_{x\to 1}\dfrac{\sqrt{x+8}-3}{\sqrt{x+3}-2}$

4. $\lim\limits_{x\to+\infty}\dfrac{(x-1)^2+1-3x}{x^2+2x^3}$

5. $\lim\limits_{x\to-\infty}\dfrac{\sqrt{x^2-1}}{x}$

6. $\lim\limits_{x\to 0}\dfrac{\sin x^2}{1-\cos x}$

7. $\lim\limits_{x\to 0}\dfrac{\sin x-\tan x}{x^3}$

8. $\lim\limits_{x\to 0}\left(1+2\tan^2 x\right)^{\cot^2 x}$

9. $\lim\limits_{x\to 0}\dfrac{\tan x-\sin x}{\sin^3 x}$

10. $\lim\limits_{x\to 0^+}\dfrac{\int_0^x t\sin t\,dt}{x^2\ln(1+x)}$

11. $\lim\limits_{x\to 0}\dfrac{\int_0^x \ln(1+t)\,dt}{x\left(e^x-1\right)}$

12. Find the following limits:

(1) $\lim\limits_{x\to\pi}\dfrac{1+\cos x}{\tan^2 x}$

(2) $\lim\limits_{x\to 0}\dfrac{\tan x-x}{x^2\tan x}$

(3) $\lim\limits_{x\to 1}x^{\frac{1}{1-x}}$

(4) $\lim\limits_{x\to 0^+}x^{\sin x}$

(5) $\lim\limits_{x\to 0}\dfrac{1-x^2-e^{-x^2}}{x\sin^3 2x}$

(6) $\lim\limits_{x\to 0}\left(\dfrac{1}{x}-\dfrac{\cos x}{\sin x}\right)\cdot\dfrac{1}{x}$

### (2) Proof Problems

1. $f(x)$ is continuous on $[0,2]$ and $f(0)=f(2)$; prove that there exists at least one point $\xi\in[0,1]$ such that $f(\xi)=f(\xi+1)$

2. Prove that for $x\geq 1$, we have $2\arctan x+\arcsin\dfrac{2x}{1+x^2}=\pi$

3. Suppose the function $f(x)$ is continuous on the closed interval $[0,1]$ and differentiable on the open interval $(0,1)$, with $f(1)=0$; prove that in the open interval $(0,1)$ there exists at least one point $\xi$ such that $f'(\xi)=-\dfrac{1}{\xi}f(\xi)$

4. Prove that $pb^{p-1}(a-b)\leq a^p-b^p\leq pa^{p-1}(a-b)$, where $0<b<a$ and $p>1$

## 2. Derivatives and Differentials

1. Find the equations of the tangent line and the normal line to the curve $\begin{cases}x=te^{-t}\\y=e^t\end{cases}$ at the point where $t=0$.

2. Find the equation of the tangent line to the curve $y=\ln x$ that is parallel to the line $y=x$.

3. Let $y=e^x\ln x$; find $y'$.

4. Let $y=\left(\arctan\dfrac{x}{3}\right)^2$; find $y'$.

5. Let $y=\sin^2 x\cdot\sin\left(x^2\right)$; find $y'$.

6. Let $y=\log_2\left(e^x-1\right)$; find $y'$.

7. Find the equation of the tangent line to the astroid $x^{\frac{2}{3}}+y^{\frac{2}{3}}=a^{\frac{2}{3}}$ $(a>0)$ at the point $M\left(\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{4}a,\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{4}a\right)$.

8. Let the function be $y=\arctan\dfrac{1}{x}$; find $dy$ and $dy\big|_{x=1}$.

9. Let $y=y(x)$ be determined by the equation $2^{xy}=x+y$; find $dy\big|_{x=0}$.

10. Given $y=\ln^2(1-x)$, find $dy$.

11. Given $y=\int_0^x\tan t^2\,dt$, find $y'$.

## 3. Indefinite and Definite Integrals

### (1) Computing Indefinite Integrals

1. $\int\left(1-\sqrt[3]{x^2}\right)^2 dx$

2. $\int\dfrac{1}{\cos^2 x\sin^2 x}\,dx$

3. $\int\dfrac{1}{4x^2+a^2}\,dx$

4. $\int x^2 e^{-x^3}\,dx$

5. $\int\dfrac{e^{2x}}{1+e^x}\,dx$

6. $\int\sqrt{a^2-x^2}\,dx$ $(a>0)$

7. $\int xe^x\,dx$

8. $\int x\ln x\,dx$

2. $\int_0^{2\pi}\left|\sin x\right|dx$

3. $\int_{-1}^{3}\dfrac{dt}{1+t^2}$

4. $\int_{\frac{1}{e}}^{e}\left|\ln x\right|dx$

5. $\int_0^{\pi^2}\sqrt{x}\cos\sqrt{x}\,dx$

6. $\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}}e^x\cos x\,dx$

## 4. Applications of Integration (Area, Volume, Arc Length)

1. Compute the area of the region enclosed by the parabola $y^2=2x$ and the line $y=x-4$.

2. Compute the volume of the solid of revolution obtained by revolving the region enclosed by the ellipse $\dfrac{x^2}{a^2}+\dfrac{y^2}{b^2}=1$ one full turn about the $x$-axis.

3. A plane passes through the center of the base circle of a cylinder of base radius $R$ and makes an angle $\alpha$ with the base plane; compute the volume of the solid cut from the cylinder by this plane.

4. Compute the area of the region enclosed by the cardioid $\rho=a(1+\cos\theta)$ $(a>0)$.

5. Find the arc length of the cardioid $\rho=a(1+\cos\theta)$ $(a>0)$.

6. The cycloid is given by $\begin{cases}x=a(t-\sin t)\\y=a(1-\cos t)\end{cases}$ $(a>0)$ ($0\leq t\leq 2\pi$):

(1) Find the area of the region enclosed by one arch of the cycloid and the $x$-axis.

(2) Find the volumes of the solids of revolution obtained by revolving the region enclosed by one arch of the cycloid and the $x$-axis one full turn about the $x$-axis and the $y$-axis, respectively.

(3) Find the length of one arch of the cycloid.

## 5. Taylor's Formula and the Behavior of Functions

1. Find, for the function $f(x)=\sin x$, its $n$th-order Maclaurin formula with the Lagrange remainder.

2. Find, for the function $f(x)=e^x$, its $n$th-order Maclaurin formula with the Lagrange remainder.

3. Find the intervals of concavity and the inflection points of $y=\ln\left(1+x^2\right)$.

## 6. Power Series

1. Find the radius of convergence, open interval of convergence, and interval of convergence of the following power series:

(1) $\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}\dfrac{x^n}{2^n\cdot n}$

(2) $\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}\left(\sqrt{n+1}-\sqrt{n}\right)(x-2)^n$

(3) $\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}\dfrac{3^n+(-2)^n}{n}x^n$

1. Find the sum function of the power series $\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}\dfrac{x^{n+1}}{n}$, and find the sum of the series $\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}\dfrac{1}{n\cdot 2^n}$.

2. Find the sum function of the power series $\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}nx^n$, and use it to find the sum of the numerical series $\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}\dfrac{n}{2^n}$.

3. Find the sum function of $\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}\dfrac{x^n}{n(n+1)}$.
