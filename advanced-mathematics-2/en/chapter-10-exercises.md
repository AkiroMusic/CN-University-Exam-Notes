1. $I=\iint\limits_D \sqrt{1-x^2-y^2}\,\mathrm{d}\sigma$, where $D$ is the region bounded by the curve $y=\sqrt{1-x^2}$ and the line $y=0$.

---

2. Sketch each region of integration and change the order of integration in each iterated integral:
(1) $\int_{1}^{2}dx\int_{2-x}^{\sqrt{2x-x^2}} f(x,y)dy$
(2) $\int_{1}^{2}dx\int_{\frac{1}{x}}^{1} f(x,y)dy$

3. Evaluate the following double integrals:
(1) $\iint\limits_D (3x-2y)\,d\sigma$, where $D$ is the closed region bounded by the two coordinate axes and the line $x+y=2$
(2) $\iint\limits_D xy^2\,d\sigma$, where $D$ is the right half of the closed disk $x^2+y^2=4$ bounded by the $y$ axis

4. Find the volume of the solid bounded by the coordinate planes, the planes $x=4$ and $y=4$, and the paraboloid $z=x^2+y^2+1$.

5. Evaluate the following double integrals using polar coordinates:
(1) $\iint\limits_D e^{-x^2-y^2}\,\mathrm{d}\sigma$, where $D$ is the part of the unit disk in the first quadrant

(2) $\iint\limits_D (4 - x - y)\,\mathrm{d}\sigma$, where $D$ is the disk $x^2 + y^2 \le 2y$

---

6. Find the area of the part of the hemisphere $z=\sqrt{2-x^2-y^2}$ cut off by the cone $z=\sqrt{x^2+y^2}$ that lies inside the cone.

7. Find the surface area of the solid bounded by two mutually perpendicular circular cylinders $x^2+y^2=R^2$ and $x^2+z^2=R^2$ of the same base radius.
