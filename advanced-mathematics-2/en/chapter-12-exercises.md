1. Prove that the following function is a solution of the corresponding ordinary differential equation:
$y = e^x$, $e^{-x}\frac{dy}{dx} + y^2 - 2ye^x = 1 - e^{2x}$

---

2. Find the general solution of each of the following ordinary differential equations:
(1) $\frac{dy}{dx} = x(2y + 1)$
(2) $\frac{dy}{dx} = \frac{y}{x} + \sqrt{\left( \frac{y}{x} \right)^2 + 1}$
(3) $\frac{dy}{dx} = 3y - 2$
(4) $\frac{dy}{dx} = \frac{3y}{x} + \frac{x+1}{x}$
(5) $\frac{dy}{dx} = \frac{2y}{x+1} + (x+1)^3$

---

3. Find the general solution of each of the following ordinary differential equations:
(1) $\frac{d^2 y}{dx^2} + \frac{2}{1 - x} \frac{dy}{dx} = 0$

(2) $2\frac{dy}{dx} \cdot \frac{d^2 y}{dx^2} = 1$

---

4. Prove that $y = (c_1 + c_2 x)e^{-2x} + \frac{1}{8}\sin2x$ is the general solution of the equation $\frac{d^2 y}{dx^2} + 4\frac{dy}{dx} + 4y = \cos2x$.

5. Prove that $y = c_1 \cos x + c_2 \sin x - \frac{1}{3}x\cos2x + \frac{4}{9}\sin2x$ is the general solution of the equation $\frac{d^2 y}{dx^2} + y = x\cos2x$.

---

6. Find the general solution of each of the following ordinary differential equations:
(1) $\frac{d^2 y}{dx^2} - 3\frac{dy}{dx} + 2y = 0$

(2) $\frac{d^2 y}{dx^2} - 3y = 0$

(3) $\frac{d^2 y}{dx^2} + 5\frac{dy}{dx} + 4y = x$

(4) $\frac{d^2 y}{dx^2} + y = 5e^{2x}$
