1. Evaluate the following line integrals with respect to arc length:
(1) $\int_L \sqrt{y}\,\mathrm{d}s$, where $L$ is the arc of the parabola $y=x^2$ from the origin $(0,0)$ to the point $(1,1)$

(2) $\oint_\Gamma (x^2+y^2+z^2)\,\mathrm{d}s$, where $\Gamma$ is the arc of the helix $x=\cos t, y=\sin t, z=t$ corresponding to $t=0$ to $t=2\pi$

---

2. Evaluate the following line integrals with respect to coordinates:
(1) $\int_L x\mathrm{d}y - y\mathrm{d}x$, where $L$ is the arc of the cycloid $x = t - \sin t,\ y = 1 - \cos t$ from the point $(0,0)$ to the point $(2\pi,0)$

(2) $\oint_L \frac{(x+y)\mathrm{d}x - (x-y)\mathrm{d}y}{x^2+y^2}$, where $L$ is the circle $x^2+y^2=a^2\ (a>0)$ traversed counterclockwise

---

3. Evaluate the following curve integrals:
(1) $\oint_L (2xy - x^2)dx + (x + y^2)dy$, where $L$ is the positively oriented boundary curve of the closed region bounded by the parabolas $y = x^2$ and $y^2 = x$

(2) $\int_L (e^x \sin y - 2y)dx + (e^x \cos y - 2)dy$, where $L$ is the upper semicircle $(x - a)^2 + y^2 = a^2 \ (y \geq 0)$ traversed clockwise

4. Using line integrals, find the area of the region bounded by the circle $x^2 + y^2 = 2y$.

---

5. Evaluate the following surface integrals with respect to area:
(1) $\iint_\Sigma \sqrt{1+4z}\, \mathrm{d}S$, where $\Sigma$ is the part of the paraboloid $z = x^2 + y^2$ with $z \leq 1$

(2) $\iint_\Sigma \frac{1}{x^2 + y^2 + z^2}\, \mathrm{d}S$, where $\Sigma$ is the part of the cylinder $x^2 + y^2 = R^2 \ (R > 0)$ between the planes $z = 0$ and $z = H \ (H > 0)$

---

6. Evaluate the following surface integrals with respect to coordinates:
(1) $\iint_\Sigma z \,\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y$, where $\Sigma$ is the lower side of the cone $z = \sqrt{x^2 + y^2} \ (0 \leq z \leq 1)$

(2) $\iint_\Sigma (x + 1)\mathrm{d}y\mathrm{d}z + y\mathrm{d}z\mathrm{d}x + \mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y$, where $\Sigma$ is the entire boundary surface of the spatial region bounded by the planes $x=0, y=0, z=0, x+y+z=1$, oriented outward

---

7. Evaluate the following surface integrals using Gauss's theorem:
(1) $\oiint_\Sigma 2xz\mathrm{d}y\mathrm{d}z + yz\mathrm{d}z\mathrm{d}x - z^2\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y$, where $\Sigma$ is the outer side of the boundary surface of the region bounded by the cone $z = \sqrt{x^2 + y^2}$ and the hemisphere $z = \sqrt{2 - x^2 - y^2}$

(2) $\iint_\Sigma x\mathrm{d}y\mathrm{d}z + y\mathrm{d}z\mathrm{d}x + z\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y$, where $\Sigma$ is the upper side of the upper hemisphere $z = \sqrt{1 - x^2 - y^2}$
