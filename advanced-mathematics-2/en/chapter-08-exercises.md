1. Given that vector $\boldsymbol{b}$ is collinear with $\boldsymbol{a}=2\boldsymbol{i}-\boldsymbol{j}+2\boldsymbol{k}$ and $\boldsymbol{a} \cdot \boldsymbol{b} = -18$, find the vector $\boldsymbol{b}$.

2. Given $\|\boldsymbol{a}\|=4$, $\|\boldsymbol{b}\|=2$, $\|\boldsymbol{a}-\boldsymbol{b}\|=2\sqrt{7}$, find the angle between $\boldsymbol{a}$ and $\boldsymbol{b}$.

3. Find the area of the triangle with vertices $A(1,-1,1)$, $B(-1,0,2)$, $C(2,-2,1)$, and the height on side $AB$.

---

4. Given two points $M_1(3,-1,2)$ and $M_2(4,-2,-1)$, find the plane through $M_1$ perpendicular to $M_1M_2$.

5. Find the angle between each of the following pairs of planes:
$2x-3y+6z-12=0$, $x+2y+2z-7=0$

6. Find the distance between each of the following pairs of parallel planes:
$19x-4y+8z+21=0$, $19x-4y+8z+42=0$

7. Compute the distance from the point to the plane:
$M(1,2,-3)$, $\Pi:5x-3y+z+4=0$

---

8. Find the equations of the following lines:
(1) the line through the point $(1,0,-2)$ perpendicular to both lines $\frac{x-1}{1} = \frac{y}{1} = \frac{z+1}{-1}$ and $\frac{x}{1} = \frac{y-1}{-1} = \frac{z}{0}$
(2) the line through the point $(2,-1,3)$ that intersects the line $\frac{x+3}{7} = \frac{y}{0} = \frac{z+6}{2}$ perpendicularly

9. Find the equations of the following planes:
(1) the plane through the point $(2,0,-1)$ containing the line $\frac{x+1}{2} = \frac{y}{-1} = \frac{z-2}{3}$
(2) the plane containing the line $\frac{x-2}{1} = \frac{y+3}{-5} = \frac{z+1}{-1}$ and parallel to the line $\begin{cases}2x-y+z-3=0 \\ x+2y-z-5=0\end{cases}$

10. Find the line through the point $(4,0,-1)$ that intersects both lines $\begin{cases}x+y+z=1 \\ 2x-y-z=2\end{cases}$ and $\begin{cases}x-y-z=3 \\ 2x+4y-z=4\end{cases}$.

11. Find the distance from the point $P(3,-1,2)$ to the line $\begin{cases}x+y-z+1=0 \\ 2x-y+z-4=0\end{cases}$.

---

12. Write the equations of the surfaces of revolution obtained by rotating the following curves about the specified axes:
(1) the circle $x^2+z^2=9$ in the $zOx$ plane rotated about the $z$ axis
(2) the parabola $z^2=2px$ in the $zOx$ plane rotated about its axis of symmetry and about the tangent line through its vertex

---

13. Find the equation of the cylinder through the curve $\begin{cases}x^2+y^2+4z^2=1 \\ x^2=y^2+z^2\end{cases}$ whose generators are parallel to the $z$ axis.

14. Find the projections of the space curve $\begin{cases}x^2+(y-1)^2+(z-1)^2=1 \\ x^2+y^2+z^2=1\end{cases}$ onto the three coordinate planes.
