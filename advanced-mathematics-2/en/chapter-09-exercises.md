1. Find each of the following limits:
(1) $\lim\limits_{(x,y)\to(0,1)} \dfrac{1-xy}{x^2+y^2}$
(2) $\lim\limits_{(x,y)\to(0,0)} \dfrac{2-\sqrt{xy+4}}{xy}$
(3) $\lim\limits_{(x,y)\to(0,0)} \dfrac{1-\cos(x^2+y^2)}{(x^2+y^2)e^{x^2y^2}}$

---

2. Find the partial derivatives of the following functions:
(1) $z=\ln\left(\tan\dfrac{x}{y}\right)$
(2) $u=x^{\frac{y}{z}}$

3. Find $\dfrac{\partial^2 z}{\partial x^2}$, $\dfrac{\partial^2 z}{\partial y^2}$ and $\dfrac{\partial^2 z}{\partial x\partial y}$ for the function
$z=y^x$

4. Let $f(x,y,z)=xy^2+yz^2+zx^2$; find $f_{xx}(0,0,1)$, $f_{xx}(1,0,2)$, $f_{yz}(0,-1,0)$ and $f_{zzx}(2,0,1)$.


---

5. Find the total differentials of the following functions:
(1) $z=\sin(x\cos y)$
(2) $z=e^{\frac{y}{x}}$

6. Find the total differential of the following function at each of the given points:
$z=\dfrac{x}{\sqrt{x^2+y^2}}$ at the points $(1,0)$ and $(0,1)$

---

7. Let $z=e^{x-2y}$, where $x=\sin t$ and $y=t^3$; find $\dfrac{dz}{dt}$.

8. Let $z=(x^2+y^2)^{xy}$; find $\dfrac{\partial z}{\partial x}$ and $\dfrac{\partial z}{\partial y}$.

9. Find the first-order partial derivatives of the following function (where $f$ has continuous first-order partial derivatives):
$u=f(x^2-y^2,xy)$

10. Let $u=f(x+y+z,x^2+y^2+z^2)$, where $f$ has continuous second-order partial derivatives; find $\Delta u=\dfrac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2}+\dfrac{\partial^2 u}{\partial y^2}+\dfrac{\partial^2 u}{\partial z^2}$.

---

11. Let $\sin y + e^x - xy^2 = 0$; find $\dfrac{dy}{dx}$.

12. Let $\dfrac{x}{z} = \ln\dfrac{z}{y}$; find $\dfrac{\partial z}{\partial x}$ and $\dfrac{\partial z}{\partial y}$.

13. Let $e^z - xyz = 0$; find $\dfrac{\partial^2 z}{\partial x^2}$.

14. Find the derivatives or partial derivatives of the functions determined by the following systems of equations:
(1) Let $\begin{cases} z = x^2 + y^2 \\ x^2 + 2y^2 + 3z^2 = 20 \end{cases}$; find $\dfrac{dy}{dx}$ and $\dfrac{dz}{dx}$.
(3) Let $\begin{cases} u = f(ux, v + y) \\ v = g(u - x, v^2y) \end{cases}$, where $f, g$ have continuous first-order partial derivatives; find $\dfrac{\partial u}{\partial x}$ and $\dfrac{\partial v}{\partial x}$.

---

15. Find the tangent line and normal plane of the curve $\boldsymbol{r} = \boldsymbol{f}(t) = (t - \sin t)\boldsymbol{i} + (1 - \cos t)\boldsymbol{j} + \left(4\sin\dfrac{t}{2}\right)\boldsymbol{k}$ at the point corresponding to $t_0 = \dfrac{\pi}{2}$.

16. Find the tangent line and normal plane of the curve $\begin{cases} x^2 + y^2 + z^2 - 3x = 0 \\ 2x - 3y + 5z - 4 = 0 \end{cases}$ at the point $(1,1,1)$.

17. Find the tangent planes of the ellipsoid $x^2 + 2y^2 + z^2 = 1$ that are parallel to the plane $x - y + 2z = 0$.

---

18. Let $a,b$ be real numbers. Among the directional derivatives of the function $z=2+ax^2+by^2$ at the point $(3,4)$, the one along the direction $(-3,-4)$ is the largest, with maximum value $10$. Find $a$ and $b$.

---

19. Find the extrema of the following functions:
(1) $z = x^3 - 4x^2 + 2xy - y^2$
(2) $z = (x^2 + y^2)^2 - 2x^2 + 2y^2$
