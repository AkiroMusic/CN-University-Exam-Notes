\vdots & \vdots & \vdots & & \vdots\\

$$

### 3. Let $A$ be a square matrix of order $n$ satisfying $A^2-5A+E=0$; prove that $A-E$ is invertible, and find its inverse.

### 4. Discuss for which values of $a$ the following system: (1) has a unique solution; (2) has no solution; (3) has infinitely many solutions? And when it has infinitely many solutions, find the general solution,

$$
\begin{cases}
ax_1+x_2+x_3=0\\
x_1+ax_2+x_3=2\\
x_1+x_2+ax_3=a
\end{cases}
$$
